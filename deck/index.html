The diagnosis

Most LMSs are designed for Management.
Learning deserves its own architect.

The incumbent

Designed for administrators

Proctored rows, attendance matrices, gradebooks, RBAC, course assignment. The learner is a passive row in someone else’s dashboard. Content is warehoused, not taught.

The correction

Designed for the learner

Elms inverts the hierarchy. The primary user is the student; every management feature falls out of serving them. The interface speaks the mariner’s tongue — a chart, a compass, a beacon — not a spreadsheet.

Every learner is different.
Temperament, pace, modality, prior knowledge — the system starts there.

02 · AI at the learner’s service

Every learner, a different temperament.
Every learner, notes in their own mode.

PDF Study Notes

For the visual reader

Structured, lecture-accurate notes rendered from your released courseware. Clean Tufte-aware layout, plotted for re-reading.

Generated Podcasts

For the auditory learner

Scripted by AI, voiced to audio, grounded in the same courseware as the PDF. Listen on the bus, before the exam, on repeat.

Customised to You

For the way you think

Slower or faster, analogy-first or definition-first, math-heavy or narrative — the notes adapt to your temperament, not the other way round.

This is multimodal AI doing real work — not one-size-fits-all chat transcripts, but a course that renders itself into the shape each learner needs.

Multi-modal AI, in service of one learner

03 · Three domains, not one generalist

One chatbot can only do an average job.
We give you three — each with a domain.

◦  Socrates my Friend

Course operations

Fees, calendars, deadlines, university offices, policies — the business of study, kept warm and precise.

◦  Socrates my Philosopher

Ideas & the wider world

Connects released concepts beyond the syllabus — every claim marked [course] or [web], every answer a new trailhead.

◦  Socrates my Guide

Courseware, directly

Answers strictly from your unlocked lessons. RAG-grounded, provenance-tagged, hallucination-resistant.

24/7, all three. Each with a focused domain — together far more capable than a single generalist agent, because the boundaries between course ops, ideas, and courseware are real and worth honouring.

The pipeline

Courseware → Second Brain → Artifacts

Real B.Sc courseware (Christ University, Bangalore) is chunked, embedded, and grounded in Firestore vector search. GenKit orchestrates four production flows: guide-chat, socratic-tutor, multi-format, ingestion. Gemini 3.7 Flash is the primary LLM with a Sarvam fallback. Cloud Run hosts the service; Secret Manager locks the keys.

The hardening

What separates product from prototype

Generation-aware circuit breaker, idempotent retries with deterministic document IDs, structured error classification, 250 tests passing, accessibility (WCAG) built in from Phase 9, RAG validated at scale after a real retrieval failure at 219+ lessons.
